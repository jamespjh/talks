# Stored images and image prompts

`dilemma.png`, `fake_barns.png` and `two_futures.png` are placeholders. Generate each image in
ChatGPT, save it over the placeholder with the same name, and put the share link in the
`credit-url` of its `image-credit` shortcode in `../../index.qmd`.

The style matches the inaugural lecture's `epistemologies.png`: a richly detailed, warm,
digitally painted editorial cartoon. Start each prompt with:

> A richly detailed, warm, digitally painted editorial cartoon illustration, landscape 3:2,
> clean lines, soft daylight, diverse characters with friendly expressive faces, no logos.

## dilemma.png (slide: Europe's dilemma)

A grand classical European university building with columns, beside a railway platform.
A sleek modern high-speed train with "AI" on its side is pulling out of the station.
On the platform, the academic community is split. Some researchers sprint to board,
clutching laptops. Others stand firm with hand-lettered placards: "Copyright?", "Energy?",
"Whose values?". In the middle, an academic leader in a scarf looks anxiously between the
departing train and the protesters. A station clock shows it is later than it looks.

## fake_barns.png (slide: Fake barns)

Rolling green European farmland under a big sky, dotted with dozens of gleaming,
identical, freshly painted red barns. A cutaway or view from behind reveals that almost all
of them are flat painted facades, propped up by wooden scaffolding built from stacks of
printed computer code. Among them stands one weathered, genuine barn with hay and a cow
inside. In the foreground, a researcher with a magnifying glass and a clipboard of test
results is peering behind one of the facades.

## two_futures.png (slide: Two futures, can be pointed)

A split-panel cartoon divided by a vertical line.

Left panel, "Vibe-coded": late at night in a cluttered lab, a postdoc grins at a chat window
on a laptop. Beside them, a teetering tower of glossy papers stamped "PUBLISHED" rests on
crumbling, cracked blocks of computer code, with cracks spreading. In the background, a
dusty office door with a cobweb carries a sign: "Research Software Engineers – closed".

Right panel, "Engineered": the same postdoc works beside a research software engineer at a
bright, orderly workbench. A friendly AI robot hands them components; the engineer checks
each one with a precision gauge against a wall dashboard of green ticks. A smaller, solid
stack of papers sits on a firm stone foundation.
